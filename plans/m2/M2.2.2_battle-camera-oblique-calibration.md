## Objective

Calibrate the maintained production `BattleLab` camera from its current relatively top-down/close M2.2 framing to a deliberate elevated oblique battlefield-command view. The view must expose terrain depth and slopes while keeping formations, upright billboard troops, and authored foliage readable. This is the representative presentation baseline that M2.3 will later profile; it is not scale, optimization, or a camera-system redesign.

## Current Baseline

- `BattleCameraRig` owns presentation-only orthographic pan, zoom, reset, keyboard/mouse input, and HUD control wiring. Its `Camera3D` child in `BattleLab.tscn` currently supplies the authored default transform (`Vector3(0, 27, 31)`, pitch `-48°`) and orthographic size (`52`). Reset restores the captured authored scene state.
- The accepted fixed battlefield profiles remain open meadow and broad highland. Terrain, projection, static foliage scatter, and combat playback already use their established production paths.
- Troop `Sprite3D` instances in `BattlePlaybackController` and foliage `Sprite3D` instances in `BattlefieldTerrainView` currently use `BaseMaterial3D.BillboardModeEnum.Enabled`. In Godot 4.7 that is full camera-facing billboarding, not the required upright fixed-Y presentation. The pass must change those two production creation paths to the current Godot 4.7 C# fixed-Y billboard mode and leave all other sprite/art/material behavior alone unless a directly necessary adjustment is discovered.

## Required Behaviour

1. Keep the camera orthographic. Recalibrate its authored default position, pitch, look direction, orthographic size, and only the existing pan/zoom limits or step values when directly required by the new framing. Use the existing `BattleCameraRig` ownership and `Camera3D` scene relationship; do not introduce orbit/free-rotation controls, camera modes, or a camera framework.
2. Select the default composition by visible production inspection of both fixed profiles. It must be elevated and oblique, looking downward across the battlefield rather than nearly straight down. A roughly 30–45° downward angle from horizontal is a visual starting region, not a required literal value: choose the smallest calibrated adjustment that demonstrates the required composition at the actual M2 world scale.
3. The default frame must show meaningful near-to-far battlefield depth, visible terrain relief, and the initial opposing formations without foreground terrain or oversized foliage burying important action. It must not make the horizon dominate, introduce obvious terrain clipping, obstruct the HUD, or become impractical at the existing zoom limits.
4. Preserve the existing pan, zoom, and reset controls. Reset must restore the deliberate authored oblique default. Keep pan bounded to the battlefield and ensure the existing controls remain useful with the calibrated angle; do not add camera-derived simulation or selection behavior.
5. Use upright world-Y billboards for ordinary troop, foliage, and remains presentation where those paths currently use full billboarding. They may turn horizontally toward the camera, but must not pitch/lean toward an elevated camera. Confirm the exact supported Godot 4.7 C# enum/property from the project binding or official 4.7 documentation before editing; the expected direct replacement is the fixed-Y billboard mode, not a custom transform script or shader.
6. Preserve the current Sprite3D source textures, terrain sampler projection, foliage source mapping/calibration, scatter placement data, combat playback, effects, remains semantics, and all scene-root validation. Do not edit PNG pixels, regenerate assets, add colliders, use raycasts, or create a second presentation path.

## Visual Acceptance

Visible pinned Godot 4.7.1 Forward+ inspection of production `BattleLab` must cover both `battlelab.m2.open-meadow` and `battlelab.m2.broad-highland`. At the default frame and while exercising pan, zoom, and reset, establish all of the following:

- Faceted terrain elevation and slopes read as depth rather than a flat plan view.
- Red and blue troop placeholder rectangles read as upright cards planted at their sampler-derived bases—not narrow edge-on slivers, tilted sheets, or flat ground tokens—and their opposing rows/ranks recede naturally into depth.
- Low grass, shrubs, trees, and other authored foliage remain upright, terrain-grounded, readable, and secondary to formations; neither camera angle nor foliage hides the initial opposing forces or HUD.
- The normal production playback remains understandable through movement, formation contact, attack bumps, damage feedback, death/remains, and rout/retreat, including a terminal result.
- Camera pan, zoom, and reset work through the existing keyboard/mouse and HUD controls, stay practical at the current supported range, and reset to the calibrated default.
- Normal Forward+ runs close cleanly; inspect their reports/logs for production identity, `passed=true`, `unexpectedErrors=0`, expected M2 projection/scatter evidence, and no unexpected errors, exceptions, assertions, missing resources/nodes, or invalid references.

Screenshots/captures should show the actual production windows sufficiently to support the composition judgment. Headless/unit checks are supporting evidence only; they cannot substitute for these visible inspections.
