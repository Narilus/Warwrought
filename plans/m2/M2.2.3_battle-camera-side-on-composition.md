# M2.2.3 — Battle Camera Side-On Composition Refinement

- **Status:** READY
- **Milestone:** M2 — Procedural 2.5D Battlefield & Rendering Scale
- **Position:** A very small post-M2.2.2 presentation calibration before M2.3 profiling. It refines the default composition, not the camera system.
- **Depends on:** M2.2.2 accepted at `3680f5428945de3d4570324bdec07fbbbbf7aaf2`.
- **Authority:** `AGENTS.md`; `DESIGN.md` §§11–15 and 20; `plans/implementation_plan.md` §11; `plans/m2/README.md`; M2.2, M2.2.1, M2.2.2, and M2.3 packets; `godot-2p5d-presentation`, `deterministic-battle-authority`, and `godot-project-operations` skills.

## Objective

Refine the authored production `BattleLab` default camera from M2.2.2's along-engagement-axis framing to an elevated, orthographic side-on or modest three-quarter-side battlefield tableau. The default must make opposing battle lines read broadly across the screen and leave enough depth to distinguish ranks, terrain relief, foliage, and contact. This is the intended representative view for later M2.3 scale profiling; it is not a literal recreation of another game's camera or a broader camera redesign.

## Canonical Fixture Orientation

M2.2.3 is defined against the actual M1 fixture rather than an arbitrary world-space yaw.

| Concern | Canonical fixture fact | Composition consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Engagement / advance axis | **World Z.** Side A deploys at `(0, -20,000)` facing South (`+Z`) and advances toward `(0, +20,000)`; Side B deploys at `(0, +20,000)` facing North (`-Z`) and advances toward `(0, -20,000)`. | The current camera at X=`0`, Z=`+34` substantially looks along the engagement corridor, reading as a behind-one-army view. |
| Battle-line / lateral axis | **World X.** Both formations use eight files with 1,000-unit file spacing; files span across X while ranks occupy depth along Z. | A pure X-side view would foreshorten this width. The selected three-quarter view must still make the X/file span and rank structure visibly legible. |
| Fronts | Side A's South-facing front and Side B's North-facing front meet across Z. | View primarily across Z from a lateral X side so the opposing bodies no longer stack one behind the other, while retaining enough Z offset to communicate formation depth. |

The fixture is presentation input only for camera composition. Its deployment, facing, orders, authoritative X/Z, and committed identities must not change.

## Required Behaviour

1. Keep the maintained production camera orthographic, elevated, and oblique. Start from the accepted M2.2.2 camera path and rotate/reposition the authored default substantially away from its current along-Z view toward a view **across the Z engagement axis from the X lateral side**, looking toward the battlefield center.
2. A mathematically exact 90-degree side view is neither required nor preferred if it collapses the fixture's X-spanning fronts. Retain a modest Z offset/three-quarter view when it remains clearly more side-on than the M2.2.2 composition and makes files, ranks, foreground/background depth, or coarse-highland terrain occlusion more readable.
3. Select the smallest necessary authored combination of lateral X position, forward/back Z offset, yaw, height, pitch, orthographic size, and reset state by visible inspection of both fixed battlefield profiles. Exact values are Builder calibration choices, not normative planning constants.
4. Preserve the accepted M2.2.2 camera behavior: orthographic projection; current pan, zoom, and reset affordances; existing bounded limits/speeds unless the new orientation creates a concrete usability defect; and reset to the authored default. Do not add player-controlled rotation, orbit, camera modes, a minimap/spectator/cinematic camera, focus system, or framework.
5. Preserve `BaseMaterial3D.BillboardModeEnum.FixedY` for ordinary troop, foliage, and remains sprites. Preserve full `Enabled` billboarding for transient hit effects. Billboard creation code is out of scope unless a verified regression is discovered.
6. Preserve the current terrain sampler projection, foliage placement/mapping/calibration, fixed-Y grounding, HUD, production scene roots, playback ownership, and minimal combat visual language. Do not alter art, imports, terrain generation/mesh, scatter, report/verifier, or M1 authority.

## Human / Visible Acceptance

Visible production inspection is required for both `battlelab.m2.open-meadow` and `battlelab.m2.broad-highland`, using the pinned Godot 4.7.1 Forward+ GUI. A human may confirm composition directly; automated screenshot generation, machine image parsing, and screenshot infrastructure are explicitly not required.

At the authored default, and again while exercising the existing pan, zoom, and reset controls, establish that:

- The camera reads clearly from the lateral side or a useful three-quarter lateral position, rather than from behind one army along the Z engagement corridor.
- Red and blue battle bodies occupy a broad lateral screen composition rather than two vertical stacks directly behind one another. The chosen three-quarter offset keeps each formation's X/file span and ranks visually apparent while still making the two sides' Z separation and broad-front contact intelligible.
- Upright troop cards remain planted at sampler-derived bases and sufficiently face-readable—not edge-on slivers, tilted sheets, or flat tokens—and contact reads as broad fronts meeting.
- Terrain slopes/depth, low grass, shrubs, trees, and rocks remain legible but do not form a dominant foreground wedge or obscure the initial opposing formations, contact, or HUD.
- Movement, attack bumps, damage flashes, death/remains, rout/retreat, and terminal playback remain readable through the normal production path.
- Meadow and highland both meet the composition bar. Do not tune only for meadow, and do not redesign the intentionally coarse highland terrain to solve an ordinary camera-composition issue.
- The existing keyboard/mouse/HUD pan, zoom, and reset controls operate cleanly, remain practical, and restore the deliberate side-on/three-quarter default. Normal GUI runs close cleanly with inspected reports/logs showing `passed=true`, `unexpectedErrors=0`, and no unexpected errors, exceptions, assertions, missing resources/nodes, or invalid references.
